# Entrepreneurial Time System

**Source**: Entrepreneur's Guide to Time Management PDF (Strategic Coach)
**Extracted**: February 3, 2026

## Overview
The Entrepreneurial Time System is a powerful framework for managing time as an entrepreneur. Instead of treating every day the same, it breaks activities into **three distinct types of days**: Free Days, Focus Days, and Buffer Days.

## The Three Types of Days

### 🏖️ FREE DAYS™ - Rejuvenation

**Purpose**: Rejuvenation on all levels (physical, mental, emotional, spiritual, creative, visionary)

**Groundrules**:
- A Free Day is a 24-hour period, from midnight to midnight
- NO focused business-related thinking or reading
- NO communication with the office
- Schedule your Free Days ahead of time
- Plan vacations one year in advance

**Key Philosophy**:
- Free Days come FIRST in the system (prerequisite for high achievement)
- You're investing in your most important asset: YOU
- Smart thinkers are well-rested and have the brain power to think creatively
- Regular Free Days keep you constantly refreshed, creative, and energized

**How to Have Great Free Days**:
1. List your favorite non-work activities
2. Book fun activities in advance (don't just hang around house)
3. Schedule in calendar and protect these days as untouchable
4. Have a PLAN for the day

**Team Benefit**: When you're gone, your team learns to make decisions without you, becoming partners rather than dependants

**Quote**: "Fatigue makes cowards of us all." - Vince Lombardi

---

### 🎯 FOCUS DAYS™ - Productivity

**Purpose**: Maximum productivity and results

**Groundrules**:
- A Focus Day is a 24-hour period
- 80% of an average workday is spent on your **top three results-producing activities**
- Each Focus Day devoted to most important relationships
- Plan your Focus Days 2-3 weeks in advance
- Review your three Focus Day activities at end of each 90-day period

**Key Philosophy**:
- These are "game days" where you do what you do best
- About your best opportunities and relationships
- All about RESULTS
- When Focus Days are working, gives incredible confidence about ability to generate revenue

**Implementation**:
- Get crystal clear on your top 3 money-making activities
- Have team help protect these days
- Be rigorous about interruptions
- Use signage on door to show availability
- Postpone non-money-making activities to Buffer Days

**Success Indicators**:
- Increased revenue
- Team handling office admin
- Working only with top opportunities
- Momentum and traction in business

---

### ⚙️ BUFFER DAYS™ - Preparation

**Purpose**: Preparation and foundation-building

**Groundrules**:
- A Buffer Day is a 24-hour period
- Use these to prepare for Free and Focus Days
- Three specific uses:
  1. Cleaning up messes
  2. Delegating "stuff"
  3. Acquiring new capabilities
- You may do Focus Day activities as a bonus

**Key Philosophy**:
- Often most overlooked and least appreciated
- CRUCIAL for putting structures in place for future success
- Without this buffer zone, challenging to build foundation for greater revenue and free time
- Like an entertainer or athlete who needs to practice, rehearse, get organized

**Three Main Activities**:

**1. Clean-ups**  
Six main types of messes:
- Administrative (paperwork)
- Environmental (office space)
- Financial
- Health-related
- Legal
- Relationship-oriented

All can be a burden that dampens energy and distracts focus. Over time, you'll clean up past messes AND get better at not creating new ones.

**2. Delegations**  
Getting activities off your plate and onto someone else's

**3. New Capabilities**  
Learning new skills, tools, systems that support your Focus Days

**Initial Phase**: At start, will need MORE Buffer Days to clean up past "messes" - this is normal. One by one, as you clean up each mess, you'll feel a burst of energy and freedom. Going forward, you'll need FEWER Buffer Days, and they'll become more strategic and future-focused.

## Implementation Strategy

### Starting Out
1. **Begin with Free Days** - These come first, not as a reward but as preparation for productivity
2. **Identify your top 3 Focus Day activities** - What actually makes you money?
3. **Schedule Buffer time** to clean up existing messes and set up systems
4. **Plan 2-3 weeks ahead** - Know what type each day will be

### Weekly Planning
- At start of week, assign each day as Free, Focus, or Buffer
- Protect each day type from bleeding into others
- Review and adjust based on results

### Quarterly Review
- Evaluate your top 3 Focus Day activities (still the right ones?)
- Assess Free Day quality (truly rejuvenating?)
- Check Buffer Day effectiveness (messes decreasing?)

## Common Obstacles (From Strategic Coach)

1. **"I don't feel comfortable when I'm not working"** → Schedule Free Days and fill them with activities
2. **"I don't have a good enough team"** → Test it - they'll surprise you
3. **"What if I meet a great prospect on Free Days?"** → Contact them when you return to work
4. **"Too difficult to get started when I get back"** → Plan first week back BEFORE you leave
5. **"My team needs to reach me"** → Survey shows they want to prove they can handle it
6. **"Can't fill up an entire Focus Day"** → If 10hr workday, 8hrs on top 3 activities
7. **"Can't see how Buffer Days are valuable"** → They prep your best Focus and Free Days
8. **"Business too unpredictable"** → Schedule one and stick to it, becomes habitual
9. **"Can't afford to take time off"** → Free Days give opportunity to improve business systems
10. **"Changes required are too complicated"** → Start with one day, build from there

## The Results

**Problem (Time & Effort Economy)**:
- One day same as another
- Always thinking about work
- Strain between work and home
- Money only reward for productivity
- Continual fatigue
- Constant "stuff" and messes
- Need crisis to be excited

**Solution (Results Economy)**:
- Multiply free time, multiply income
- Sharp distinction between days
- Many other interests besides work
- Freedom from "stuff" and messes
- Increased physical and mental energy
- Harmony between work and home
- Goals, progress, achievement provide excitement
- Active career extends another 20-40 years
