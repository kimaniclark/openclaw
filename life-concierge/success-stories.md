## Restaurant Reservations Beyond OpenTable/Resy

**Date:** February 19, 2026

**Task:** Book a dinner reservation at Lightfoot Restaurant in Leesburg, VA for Saturday evening.

**Challenge:** Lightfoot doesn't use OpenTable or Resy — they use Toast Tables, a less common reservation platform.

**What Johnny Did:**
1. Found the restaurant's reservation system (Toast Tables)
2. Navigated the booking interface via browser automation
3. Selected the requested date (Feb 22), time (7:00 PM), and party size (2)
4. Filled in guest information and completed the booking
5. Captured confirmation screenshot and delivered to client

**Result:** ✅ Reservation confirmed. Client received SMS confirmation from restaurant.

**Capability Demonstrated:** Johnny can book reservations across multiple platforms — not just the big ones. If a restaurant has online booking, Johnny can figure it out and complete the reservation.

## Proactive Relationship Management

**Date:** February 22, 2026

**Task:** Keep Kimani connected with his inner circle without him having to track who to reach out to.

**Challenge:** Maintaining relationships with 123+ people is overwhelming. Important contacts slip through the cracks — birthdays get missed, months go by without connecting with close friends.

**What Johnny Did:**
1. Built a personal CRM from existing contacts (Contacts Journal + Quo imports)
2. Tracked contact frequency preferences (how often each person should be reached)
3. Identified birthdays and special dates
4. Every Sunday, analyzed the contact database to suggest 2-3 priority outreach targets
5. Flagged **Dhani Jones** for his birthday (Feb 22), plus two "feel good" contacts who boost Kimani's mood

**Suggested Outreach:**
- Dhani Jones — Birthday message
- Chela — Old friend, "feel good" contact at Lionsgate
- Adam Schneider — Good friend, local to Potomac MD

**Result:** ✅ Kimani messaged all three. Said it was "INCREDIBLY helpful."

**Capability Demonstrated:** Johnny acts as a relationship manager — tracking who matters, when to reach out, and surfacing the right people at the right time. No one falls through the cracks.
