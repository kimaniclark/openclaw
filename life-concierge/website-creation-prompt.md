## Website Requirements

### Design Style
Clean, premium, trustworthy. Think high-end consulting meets modern tech. Inspiration: Framer templates, premium SaaS sites. Not corporate — confident and results-focused.

### Key Pages

1. **Homepage** — Hero value prop, social proof, clear CTA
2. **How It Works** — Process overview, onboarding flow
3. **Life Areas** — Showcase all 22 areas with examples
4. **Success Stories** — Real client examples (anonymized)
5. **Pricing** — Consultation-based (no public pricing shown)
6. **Contact / Book Consultation**

### Tone
Confident, premium, results-focused. Avoid corporate jargon. Speak to people who value their time highly and want life transformation, not just organization.

### Primary Call-to-Action
**"Schedule Your Life Concierge Consultation"** — not a generic "Get Started"

### Color Scheme
Professional but approachable — deep blues or rich neutrals, clean whites, accent colors for the life area categories.
