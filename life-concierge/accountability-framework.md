# Life Concierge Accountability Framework

**Source:** 200+ Item Accountability Spreadsheet
**Total Items:** 1,217 rows across 23+ life areas
**Model:** RACI accountability (Accountable, Responsible, Consulted, Informed)

## Onboarding Philosophy

1. **Quick wins first (48-72h)** — solve something immediate to build trust
2. **Priority-driven assessment** — work through sections based on client's pain points
3. **Spread over 2-4 weeks** — don't overwhelm, integrate gradually
4. **Voice message responses** — friction-free input for busy executives

## RACI Model

For each item, Life Concierge defines:
- **Accountable:** Who owns the outcome (usually client)
- **Responsible:** Who does the work (Johnny/team)
- **Consulted:** Who provides input
- **Informed:** Who needs to know

This ensures nothing falls through the cracks while maintaining appropriate client oversight.
