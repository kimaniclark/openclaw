# Travel Agent Referral Email Template

**To:** [FRIEND_EMAIL], kristin@lilypondluxury.com
**From:** assistant@kimaniclark.com
**Subject:** Introduction: [FRIEND_NAME] ↔ Kristin Sakowski

---

Hi Kristin and [FRIEND_NAME],

I wanted to connect you two!

**Kristin** — [FRIEND_NAME] is a [RELATIONSHIP/CONTEXT]. [He/She/They] [TRAVEL_NEED_OR_CONTEXT].

**[FRIEND_NAME]** — Kristin is my travel agent at Lily Pond Travel Agency. She's been fantastic for [specific examples if relevant — luxury trips, complex itineraries, etc.]. I think she'd be a great resource for you.

I'll let you two take it from here!

Best,
Kimani

---

## Variables to fill:
- `[FRIEND_NAME]` — Name of person being referred
- `[FRIEND_EMAIL]` — Their email address
- `[RELATIONSHIP/CONTEXT]` — e.g., "good friend," "colleague," "someone I met at [event]"
- `[TRAVEL_NEED_OR_CONTEXT]` — e.g., "is planning a trip to Italy," "travels frequently for work," "looking for a reliable travel agent"
- `[He/She/They]` — Appropriate pronoun

## Usage
Johnny can draft this email with the friend's details and send via Gmail.
