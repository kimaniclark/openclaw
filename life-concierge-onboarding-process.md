# Life Concierge - Onboarding Process

The proven methodology for onboarding new Life Concierge customers.

---

## 🎯 Phase 1: Quick Wins (Days 1-3)

**Goal**: Demonstrate immediate value and build trust before comprehensive assessment.

### Discovery Call
**Before diving into 200 questions**, ask the prospect:

**Key Questions:**
1. "What are a few obvious pain points in your life right now?"
2. "What pet peeves or frustrations keep coming up?"
3. "If you could get 3 things off your plate immediately, what would they be?"

**Examples of Common Responses:**
- "I never remember to schedule my car maintenance"
- "I have no time to find entertainment/events I'd enjoy"
- "My closet is a mess and I waste time deciding what to wear"
- "I forget to follow up with important contacts"
- "My home repairs pile up because I don't coordinate contractors"

### Immediate Action & Parallel Discovery
Take 1-3 of those pain points and **solve them within 48-72 hours**.

**Important**: You can **start the 200-question framework in parallel** while working on quick wins. Don't wait for wins to be completed before beginning assessment. This maximizes efficiency and client engagement.

**Example Timeline:**
- Day 1: Discovery call, identify 3 quick wins, begin questions on first 2 life areas
- Day 2: Continue working quick wins, ask questions on 2 more life areas
- Day 3: Deliver first quick win, continue assessment questions
- Day 4: Deliver remaining quick wins, have 6-8 life areas mapped

This approach keeps momentum high and shows you're working comprehensively while delivering immediate value.

### Quick Win Examples

**Example Quick Wins:**
- Schedule overdue car maintenance
- Set up entertainment tracking for their cities/artists
- Create simple home repair tracking system
- Send pending follow-up emails they've been avoiding
- Book restaurant reservations they've been meaning to make

**Why This Works:**
- Client sees value before paying for full onboarding
- Builds trust ("they actually did something immediately")
- Demonstrates competence
- Creates momentum for deeper work
- Reduces buyer's remorse / increases commitment

## 📋 Phase 2: Comprehensive Assessment (Weeks 1-4)

**After quick wins are delivered**, begin the 200+ question framework across 20+ life areas.

### The 200-Question Framework

**Major Life Areas (20+):**
1. Time Management
2. Health
3. Fitness
4. Mindset
5. Emotional Health
6. Vision/Purpose
7. Food
8. Home/Environment
9. Car
10. Fashion
11. Technology
12. Relationships
13. Social
14. Sex
15. Fun
16. Lifestyle
17. Entertainment
18. Travel
19. Financial
20. Business/Career
21. Communications
22. Online Presence
23. Personal Development
24. Innovation
25. Contribution

**Assessment Method:**
- Spread questions over multiple sessions (not all at once)
- 2-3 areas per week
- Natural conversation, not interrogation
- Client can voice message responses (friction-free)
- Mix of structured questions + open-ended discovery

**200 Sub-Areas:**
Comprehensive spreadsheet maps all aspects of personal life (the "secret IP")

---

## 🏗️ Phase 3: System Build (Weeks 2-6)

Based on assessment, build out custom systems:

**Dashboard Creation:**
- Start with 3-5 most important life areas
- Add sections incrementally as work progresses
- Visual tracking for key metrics
- Link to relevant resources

**Service Coordination:**
- Identify and document all service providers
- Create contact lists and schedules
- Set up recurring maintenance reminders
- Coordinate with home assistants, VAs, contractors

**Automation Setup:**
- Calendar integration
- Email management workflows
- Entertainment tracking
- Travel coordination
- Health/fitness tracking

**Documentation:**
- Contact lists (financial advisors, doctors, contractors, etc.)
- Preferences and decision frameworks
- Standard operating procedures
- Emergency contacts and protocols

---

## 🎨 Phase 4: Refinement (Ongoing)

**Continuous Improvement:**
- Add new life areas to dashboard as tackled
- Refine systems based on usage
- Identify new pain points and solve proactively
- Quarterly life area reviews
- Annual comprehensive assessment update

---

## 💡 Onboarding Best Practices

### Do's:
✅ Start with quick wins (demonstrate value immediately)  
✅ Use voice messages for discovery (reduces client friction)  
✅ Build dashboard incrementally (not overwhelming)  
✅ Document everything for future reference  
✅ Proactive problem-solving (don't wait to be asked)  
✅ Regular check-ins during onboarding  
✅ Celebrate wins and progress  

### Don'ts:
❌ Don't dump 200 questions at once  
❌ Don't build systems without understanding context  
❌ Don't wait for client to ask for help  
❌ Don't create systems client won't use  
❌ Don't skip the quick wins phase  
❌ Don't over-complicate early on

## 🎯 The "Aha" Moment

**When does the client truly "get it"?**

Usually happens in Phase 1 (Quick Wins) when they realize:
1. You actually solved something in 48 hours they've been putting off for months
2. The solution is better than they would have done themselves
3. It required zero effort from them
4. You anticipated needs they hadn't articulated

**Example:**
Client mentions they've been meaning to schedule car maintenance. Within 24 hours:
- Johnny looked up their car service history
- Found overdue items
- Researched and selected a highly-rated mechanic
- Got 3 quotes
- Scheduled appointment during client's free time
- Added to calendar with all details
- Set future maintenance reminders

Client thinks: "Holy shit, I would have spent 3 hours on this and put it off another month. They just... did it."

That's the moment they understand the value.
