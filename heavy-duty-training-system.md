# Heavy Duty Training System

## Overview
Heavy Duty is a high-intensity, low-volume training methodology developed by **Mike Mentzer**. It emphasizes maximum effort in minimal time, requiring specialized coaching and proper form.

## Training Philosophy
- High-intensity, low-volume approach
- Each set taken to absolute failure
- Longer recovery periods between workouts
- Focus on progressive overload
- Requires strict form and mental intensity
