## Posting Tips

1. **Photos:** Use existing eBay photos - FB allows up to 10 per listing
2. **Respond fast:** FB buyers expect quick replies
3. **"Is this available?":** Just reply "Yes!" and wait for real questions
4. **Lowballers:** Polite "Price is firm" or "I can do $X" 
5. **No-shows:** Common on FB. Don't hold without deposit.
6. **Payment:** Cash at pickup or Venmo before they arrive
