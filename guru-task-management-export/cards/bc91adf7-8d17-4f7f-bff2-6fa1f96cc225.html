You can create a task with a "backlog" status.

The backlog is a place where you can store tasks that you aren't ready to schedule in your calendar yet. Once you're ready to schedule a backlog task in your calendar, you can add a deadline to the task, change the status to "Auto-scheduled", and then Motion will schedule it in your calendar. You can find your backlogged tasks in My Tasks → Projects → Change "Active Projects" view to "Backlog"

Note: you may need to change the task filters in order to show backlogged tasks and not just backlogged projects.Hope this helps. Let me know if there's anything else I can assist you with.
