SOP: Task Management in Motion

Objective:
To effectively put a task in motion using the Motion task management system.

Key Steps:

  1. Access the Motion platform and navigate to the appropriate project in the workspace.
  2. Add a new task by selecting "Add Task" and provide relevant details such as task name, notes, estimated duration, due date, and schedule.
  3. Set the task priority to determine the order of completion.
  4. Specify if the task has a hard or soft deadline.
  5. Save the task to auto-schedule it, which will add it to the calendar for tracking.
  6. Mark the task as done upon completion.
  7. For recurring tasks, set up a schedule for automatic reoccurrence.
  8. Add any comments or tag team members for collaboration.

Cautionary Notes:

  • Ensure all tasks added to Slack or other platforms are reflected in Motion for consistency.
  • Double-check task details and scheduling to avoid errors in task management.
  • Communicate clearly with team members regarding task assignments and updates.

Tips for Efficiency:

  • Utilize the auto-scheduling feature to streamline task management and calendar integration.
  • Regularly review and update tasks to stay organized and on track.
  • Encourage team members to provide timely updates and comments for effective collaboration.

By following these steps, you can effectively manage tasks in Motion and ensure smooth workflow and task completion within your team.
