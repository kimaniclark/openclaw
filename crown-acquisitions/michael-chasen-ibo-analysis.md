## 🔥 **Value Proposition - Why IBO Beats Private Equity**

### **1. Tax Elimination (The Game Changer) 💰**
- **Section 1042 Rollover:** Defer/eliminate capital gains taxes entirely
- **S-Corp ESOP:** Zero federal and state income tax permanently
- **The Math:** $50M company → PE sale nets ~$37.5M after taxes, IBO nets $57.9M (**54% improvement**)

### **2. Control Retention 🎯**
- No PE board seats or veto rights
- Existing management stays intact
- 30-49% minority sale keeps full operational control

### **3. Clean Structure ✅**
- No earn-out risk (vs PE performance targets)
- No forced equity rollover (vs PE 20-30% requirement)
- Optional warrants for upside participation
