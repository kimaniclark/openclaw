# Roland Frasier's Initial Acquisition Call Framework

*Source: Epic Network course - advice for initial calls with people you don't know*

## The 4-Point Framework

### 1. **Business Story (Rapport Building)**
**Ask:** *"Would you please tell me the story of this business? How did it come about? How did you get involved with it?"*

**Purpose:** Get them talking, build rapport, let them be the expert

---

### 2. **Entrepreneurial Journey**
**Ask:** *"How did you get into this business? Tell me about your entrepreneurial journey."*

**Purpose:** Personal connection, understand their background and motivations

---

### 3. **Common Touch Points (Interject Friendly)**
**Do:** When they mention interests/experiences you share (fishing, sports, location, etc.) → jump in and connect

**Purpose:** Build personal connection, establish commonality

---

### 4. **Future Pacing**
**Ask:** 
- *"Have you ever thought about selling this business?"*
- *"If you did sell, what would that look like?"*
- *"What would life look like after?"*

**Purpose:** Get them envisioning a future without the business, plant the seed
