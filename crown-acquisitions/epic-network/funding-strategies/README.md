## Overview

This folder contains Roland Frasier's comprehensive guide to 216+ strategies for acquiring businesses with $0 out-of-pocket. These strategies are color-coded into three categories:

- 🟢 **Green** = Pre-LOI/Pre-Closing Strategies
- 🟡 **Yellow** = Both Pre & Post-Closing Strategies  
- 🔵 **Blue** = Post-Closing Strategies

1. **Deal details**: Purchase price, seller motivation, assets involved
2. **Your resources**: Cash available, credit, relationships, skills
3. **Constraints**: Timeline, risk tolerance, seller requirements

The AI can then recommend applicable strategies from this list.
