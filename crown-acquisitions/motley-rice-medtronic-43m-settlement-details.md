## 🎯 **The Fraud: INFUSE Bone Graft Scandal**

### **What Medtronic Did Wrong:**

**1. Ghost-Written Medical Articles**
- Medtronic employees **secretly wrote or edited** research articles published in respected medical journals
- These "independent" articles promoted INFUSE Bone Graft
- Concealed Medtronic's authorship to appear as unbiased medical research

**2. Hidden Financial Relationships**
- Paid physician authors **"hundreds of millions of dollars"** in "consulting fees"
- These payments were designed to induce physician acceptance and generate sales growth
- Financial conflicts of interest were not properly disclosed in published studies

**3. Concealed Adverse Effects**
- **Omitted known facts** about INFUSE's dangerous side effects observed in clinical trials
- Particularly dangerous when used "off-label" for cervical spine fusion surgery
- Known complications included severe swelling, difficulty swallowing, and other serious injuries

**4. Fraudulent Clinical Trial Design**
- Manipulated clinical trial design and reporting of clinical data
- Presented skewed data to make INFUSE appear safer and more effective than it actually was
