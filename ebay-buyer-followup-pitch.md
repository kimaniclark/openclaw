## Phone Script (If Call Happens)

**Opening:**
"Hey [Name]! Thanks for hopping on. How's the home gym treating you?"

**Transition:**
"So, the delivery coordination we did - that's actually a small example of what Life Concierge does full-time. I'm curious, when it comes to managing your personal life, what's your current setup? Do you have anyone helping you with stuff or is it all DIY?"

**Pain Point Discovery:**
- "What takes up most of your time that you wish you could delegate?"
- "Have you ever hired a VA or assistant before?"
- "If yes: What worked? What didn't?"
- "What would it be worth to you to never think about [their pain point] again?"

**Value Proposition:**
"Here's what Life Concierge does differently:
1. **Comprehensive:** 20+ life areas, not just calendar + email
2. **Reliable:** AI never forgets, never drops balls (like your 2-week wait)
3. **Proactive:** We handle things before you have to ask
4. **Always on:** 24/7 availability, instant response
5. **Coordinated:** Everything connected (travel → calendar → transportation)"

**Social Proof:**
"You're actually the perfect case study - you experienced the difference between human VA (2 weeks) and AI assistance (same day). That reliability is what our clients pay for."

**Pricing:**
"We're in beta right now at $600-1000/month depending on complexity. That replaces what you'd pay for human VAs ($800-1000/month at $5-7/hour) but with way better service. And for reference, people were paying $5K just for a COURSE teaching them how to hire VAs themselves."

**Close:**
"Would you want to try it for a month and see how it works for you? First month we can do at $400 since you're coming from the eBay experience."

**Objection Handling:**
- **"That's expensive"** → "Compare to hiring a human VA at $5-7/hour plus your time managing them. This is better service, lower cost, zero management overhead."
- **"I need to think about it"** → "Totally fair. Want me to send you the dashboard link so you can see what clients actually get?"
- **"I don't have that much going on"** → "The delivery coordination alone would have saved you hours of research and phone calls. Imagine that across every area of your life."
