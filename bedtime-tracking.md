## Condition: Delayed Sleep Phase Syndrome (DSPS)

**What it is**: Circadian rhythm disorder where natural sleep-wake cycle is delayed. Body naturally wants to sleep very late and wake up late.

**Key Insight**: DSPS is correctable with intentionality and the right systems. Not a permanent condition - it's behavioral and can be shifted.

## Strategies to Shift Bedtime Earlier

### Light-Based Interventions
1. **Morning bright light exposure** (first hour awake)
   - Resets circadian clock earlier
   - Use sunlight or light therapy box
   
2. **Evening light reduction** (2 hours before target bedtime)
   - Dim lights in home
   - Blue light blocking glasses
   - No screens 1 hour before bed

### Schedule-Based Interventions
1. **Gradual shift** (15-30 min earlier every few days)
   - Don't try to jump 7 hours immediately
   - Sustainable, gradual progress
   
2. **Wake time anchor** (more important than bedtime)
   - Set consistent wake time FIRST
   - Body will naturally get tired earlier
   - Even if sleep-deprived first few days

3. **No napping** during transition
   - Makes it harder to sleep at night
   - Stay awake until target bedtime

### Behavioral Interventions
1. **Evening wind-down routine** (start 1 hour before bed)
   - Brain dump journal
   - Chamomile tea
   - Reading (paper book, not screen)
   - Meditation
   
2. **Bedroom is for sleep only**
   - No work in bedroom
   - No phone in bedroom after lights out
   - Creates mental association: bedroom = sleep
   
3. **Physical exhaustion**
   - Exercise earlier in day (not close to bedtime)
   - Jiu Jitsu, working out makes body need sleep

### Social/Accountability Interventions
1. **Bedtime reminders** (Johnny can send)
   - 2 hours before: "Wind-down starts soon"
   - 1 hour before: "Start bedroom routine"
   - Target time: "Lights out!"
   
2. **Morning accountability**
   - Report wake time to Johnny
   - Track consistency
   - Celebrate wins
