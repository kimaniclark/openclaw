## Purpose
This 200-item spreadsheet is the **core IP** for Life Concierge client onboarding and discovery:
- Maps all 23 life areas with ~200 sub-areas
- Defines accountability (EA, Personal, Condo Assistant, etc.)
- Foundation for comprehensive client assessment

## Structure

## Onboarding Strategy

### Phase 1: Quick Wins (48-72 hours)
- Start with 1-3 high-impact areas the client mentions
- Demonstrate value immediately
- Build trust before comprehensive assessment

### Phase 2: Comprehensive Discovery (2-4 weeks)
- Use this 200-item framework to systematically assess ALL life areas
- Not all items will apply to every client (some marked with * or left blank)
- Identify gaps, pain points, and opportunities
- Create personalized dashboard sections

### Phase 3: Ongoing Management
- Dashboard becomes single source of truth
- Regular check-ins on each area
- Proactive maintenance and optimization
