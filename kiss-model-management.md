## Business Model

**Target Clients**: Brands, companies, advertising agencies that need models for shoots/campaigns

**Lead Generation**:
- Apollo.io (marketer sources leads)
- Inbound website contact form
- Other sources (TBD with marketer)

**Sales Process**:
1. Lead generation from Apollo.io
2. Email outreach to potential clients
3. Book discovery calls
4. Client requests specific model types
5. Create packages in PortfolioPad (curated groups of models)
6. Send proposals to client
7. Close deal and book models
