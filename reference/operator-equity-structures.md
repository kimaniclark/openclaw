# Operator/CEO Equity Structures & Protections

Reference doc for hiring operators to run ventures.

---

## Standard Vesting

**4-year vest, 1-year cliff:**
- Nothing vests until 12 months (cliff) — they have to prove themselves
- 25% vests at cliff
- Remaining 75% vests monthly over 3 years
- If they leave/fired before year 1, they walk with $0

---

## Equity Protections

| Protection | What it does |
|------------|--------------|
| **Cliff** | Nothing vests until 12 months |
| **Double-trigger acceleration** | Equity only accelerates if BOTH acquisition happens AND they're terminated (prevents early cash-out) |
| **Buyback rights** | If terminated for cause, you can buy back vested shares at low price (or $0) |
| **Restricted stock with repurchase** | More control than options — you can repurchase unvested shares if they leave |
| **Board control** | Keep board majority so you can fire if needed |
| **Milestone/KPI-based vesting** | Vest based on hitting targets (revenue, launch, user goals) instead of just time |

---

## KPI-Based Vesting (Preferred)

Instead of pure time-based vesting, tie equity to performance:

**Example structure:**
- 25% vests at Year 1 cliff (standard)
- Remaining 75% tied to milestones:
  - 15% at MVP launch
  - 20% at $X revenue or Y users
  - 20% at profitability or Series A
  - 20% at sustained growth target

**Why it's better:**
- Aligns incentives with outcomes
- Bad operator can't coast for 4 years
- Good operator earns equity faster by hitting goals
- "Hard to lose" — you only give equity for results

## Typical Operator Equity Ranges

| Role | Equity Range | Notes |
|------|--------------|-------|
| CEO/Co-founder equivalent | 15-25% | Full-time, leading everything |
| Fractional CEO/CPO | 5-10% | Part-time (10-20 hrs/week) |
| Technical co-founder | 20-40% | If building core product |
| Operator with your funding | 10-20% | You provide capital, they execute |

## Where to Find Operators

**Active networks:**
- **YC Co-Founder Matching** — https://www.ycombinator.com/cofounder-matching (free, 100K+ matches)
- **Indie Hackers** — https://www.indiehackers.com (builders seeking equity opportunities)
- **South Park Commons** — https://southparkcommons.com (SF founder community)
- **Contrary Talent** — https://contrary.com (VC talent network)
- **Twitter/X** — "Looking for operator/co-founder" posts work

**For more serious ventures:**
- **Search fund operators** — Stanford GSB, HBS programs train operators to run companies
- **EIR programs** — Partner with a VC or run your own Entrepreneur-in-Residence program
- **Executive recruiters** — Riviera Partners, True Search (expensive but proven talent)

---

## The Pitch That Attracts Good Operators

1. **Funded** — even self-funded means no fundraising distraction
2. **Clear idea with validation** — not just napkin sketch
3. **Autonomy** — they run it, you advise
4. **Real equity** — 10-20%, not token 1-2%
5. **Your track record** — why should they bet on your idea?

---

## Red Flags in Operators

- Can't articulate what they'd do in first 90 days
- Want salary-heavy, equity-light comp
- No references from people who worked *for* them
- Vague on metrics from past roles
- Resistant to milestone-based vesting
