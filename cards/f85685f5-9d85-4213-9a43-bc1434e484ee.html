Complete Your Inspection on Your Schedule

 

DC DMV has two self-service inspection kiosks, which allow District residents to perform their own vehicle emissions tests, 24-hours, seven days per week. Residents with vehicles year 2005 and newer, who have previously had their vehicles inspected at the DMV Inspection Station, are invited to visit the kiosks located in:

 

  • Ward 4: Takoma Recreation Center

  • Ward 8: Fort Stanton Recreation Center

 

The inspection fee is $35.00 for a two-year period. Failure to renew your inspection prior to the expiration date will result in a penalty fee of $20.00 per 1-to-30-day period, up to $480.00. A $35.00 re-inspection fee applies after expiration of the original 20-day re-inspection period or if more than two re-inspections are required within a 20-day period. Payments for all inspection fees must be made at the time of registration renewal.
