### Life Concierge Onboarding Methodology (3:24 AM)

**Quick Wins Strategy** - Critical onboarding insight:

**Phase 1: Quick Wins (BEFORE 200 questions)**
- Ask for 2-3 obvious pain points / pet peeves / things to get off their plate
- Examples: "never schedule car maintenance", "closet is a mess", "forget to follow up"
- **Solve 1-3 of those within 48-72 hours**
- Demonstrates immediate value
- Builds trust before comprehensive assessment
- Reduces buyer's remorse
- Creates momentum

**Phase 2: Comprehensive Assessment**
- Then begin the 200-question framework
- Spread over weeks (not all at once)
- Voice message responses (friction-free)
- Natural conversation style

**Why This Works:**
- Client sees value IMMEDIATELY (not weeks later)
- "Holy shit, they actually did it" moment
- Trust established before deep dive
- Increases commitment and engagement

**The Psychology:**
Client has been putting off some task for months. You solve it in 24 hours with zero effort from them. That's when they understand the value proposition.
