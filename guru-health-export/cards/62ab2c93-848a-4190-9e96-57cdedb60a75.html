Hello,
Thank you for completing your patient questionnaire for your upcoming GI procedure, we greatly appreciate this. We have reviewed your information and need to provide you with some important arrival instructions. 

  • Please arrive to the surgery center at 11:15am. Your procedure is scheduled to start at 12:45pm. Please note the building opens at 6:00am. Your time is an estimate from your provider’s office and delays may be possible.
  • Items to bring on the day of your procedure: Photo ID, insurance card, and if you were notified of co-payments then please bring a form of payment. 
  • Please follow all the diet/prep instructions from your provider and contact their office directly for any questions.
  • Please hydrate well 1-2 days prior to your procedure. 
  • You may drink water or Gatorade/electrolyte drinks (sugar-free if diabetic and not red, purple, or blue in coloring if you are having a colonoscopy) on the morning of your procedure until four hours prior to your procedure start time (stop at 8:45am), and then nothing else by mouth. Orally hydrating prior to your procedure helps to reduce post-procedure nausea. This may be different from the instructions you receive from the office.
  • Please do not take any vitamins/supplements or any NSAIDs (Ibuprofen, Advil, Motrin, Aleve, Naproxen) 7 days before your procedure.
  • Dress in loose, clean, comfortable clothes with sturdy shoes. Think easy on/off.
  • Shower/shampoo prior to arrival either the night before or the morning of your procedure and do not apply any additional skin products to your face, chest, or arms.
  • Remove contacts and bring a case to secure glasses or other removable items such as hearing aids or dentures.
  • Leave all jewelry and valuables at home as we are not responsible for lost items. Remove all metal from the body.
  • You will need a responsible adult to accompany you home as you cannot take an Uber/Lyft/Taxi unaccompanied. We will need to verify your ride if they do not check in with you at the time of arrival.
  • We are located at 1133 21st St., NW, Suite 1000 Washington, DC 20036. Building 2 on the 10th floor. There is parking below the building, please bring your ticket in to receive partial validation. Please have a form of payment for parking.


Thank you,Nyrie, RN
