• REMEMBER that LIGHT is a DRUG that promotes WAKEFULNESS! Avoid it at night and augment it inthe morning. It is specifically the short frequency blue light portion of the light spectrum that triggers firingin the photopic receptors of the retina to cue your hypothalamus that it is light outside. Unfortunately in our24/7 "lit" society our electronics are all spilling copious blue light negating the biological period of dusk.

* It is key to block alerting blue light in the evening, after 10 PM with lenses, filters, and frequency (color)specific LED lighting

* Continue to use the orange/red lens glasses you have. If you need more you may consider the inexpensiveUvex orange lens glasses. Search for: UVEX orange lens glasses. They should be about $9 and the standardUVEX S1933x are fine unless you wear glasses in the evening in which case you may want the larger S0360xmodel. Wear these in the evening to minimize exposure to light and its alerting affects on your sleep-wakerhythm.

GOAL: wear these for TV, reading, laundry etc after 10 PM
